# Karossy Foods — Website Design Direction

## Three Candidate Approaches

### 1. Harvest Ledger
**Very Brief Intro:** An editorial, provenance-first visual language that pairs archival farm cues with crisp export logistics. It feels established, grounded, and globally literate.
**Probability:** 0.07

### 2. Solar Market
**Very Brief Intro:** A sun-washed, contemporary food world built from warm crop tones, generous typography, and documentary photography. It is energetic without becoming loud.
**Probability:** 0.04

### 3. Meridian Pantry
**Very Brief Intro:** A premium ingredients house visualized through a deep botanical field, luminous produce imagery, and map-inspired lines. It makes export feel considered, traceable, and elegant.
**Probability:** 0.08

## Chosen Approach — Meridian Pantry

**Design Movement:** Contemporary editorial commerce with African botanical modernism.

**Core Principles:**
1. Establish confidence through hierarchy, restraint, and clear provenance language rather than generic corporate visuals.
2. Let ingredient imagery feel tactile and abundant, framed against deep botanical colour and warm paper tones.
3. Use circulation and route motifs to transform export logistics into a visual story.
4. Prefer asymmetric editorial compositions, broad breathing room, and rhythmically offset panels over conventional centered grids.

**Color Philosophy:** The site balances **Canopy Green** (a dark, earthy botanical green) with sunlit saffron, papery oat, and restrained clay accents. Green establishes trust and stewardship; saffron celebrates harvest and appetite; oat gives information room to breathe; clay adds human warmth without crowding the palette.

**Layout Paradigm:** The home page moves like a trade route: a dark, immersive origin scene opens into sunlit editorial panels, then shifts between full-bleed field images, offset information modules, and a horizontal product rail. Subpages repeat a left-edge route line and generous offset title bands rather than a generic uniform card grid.

**Signature Elements:**
1. A fine dashed “meridian route” line with round waypoints.
2. Botanic silhouette marks and crop-label microtype.
3. Rounded-square image apertures with offset borders and saffron corner tags.

**Interaction Philosophy:** Movement should evoke a well-run supply chain: crisp, intentional, and reassuring. Buttons respond immediately, product tiles lift subtly, and navigation has clear active-state wayfinding. No effect should obstruct reading or disguise an action.

**Animation:** Use 180–260ms transitions with `cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)`. Editorial blocks may rise 10px and fade once when entering view; images make a restrained 1.03 scale shift on hover. The route line is revealed in a short, single pass. All nonessential motion is disabled for reduced-motion preferences.

**Typography System:** `DM Serif Display` carries expressive editorial headlines in mixed case, while `Manrope` handles labels, navigation, data points, and body copy. Headline sizes use compact line-height (0.95–1.05); microtype is uppercase with generous tracking; paragraph copy stays calm, at 15–18px with a reading-friendly 1.65 line-height.

**Brand Essence:** **Karossy Foods is a provenance-led food export partner for buyers who want vibrant ingredients, reliable movement, and a closer connection to origin.** Personality: grounded, spirited, meticulous.

**Brand Voice:** Assured and sensory, balancing flavour with operational clarity. Headlines should be direct and vivid; CTAs should describe the next relationship step rather than use generic commands.

> “Ingredients with a clear way forward.”
>
> “Ask for this season’s export brief.”

**Wordmark & Logo:** A bold, text-free “K” monogram built from two overlapping leaves and a curved trade-route stroke. The mark feels like a seed, a map pin, and a forward direction in one graphic.

**Signature Brand Color:** **Canopy Green — #153D35**

## Style Decisions

- The meridian route is a structural navigation language on every interior page: dashed side spines, circular waypoints and visible handover sequences guide the reader through the content.
- Product and export pages use tactile ingredient photography and documentary crop moments alongside commerce information to remain sensory and provenance-led.
- The Karossy mark is treated as a botanical forward-route monogram and recurs as a seal or waypoint where appropriate.

- Product-detail placeholders are composed as **Karossy export dossiers**: branded image fields, route captions and restrained pack-size framework labels avoid an administrative spreadsheet feel while retaining clear editorial guidance for future approved content.

- The product index acts as a **Meridian Route**: a dashed left spine and saffron waypoints make each category a visible handover, while compact product apertures bring ingredient abundance into the discovery stage.

- Product dossiers use a **full-width image-and-brief spread**: oversized category photography, generous editorial title space and two grouped commercial detail panels make each category feel like a considered sourcing opportunity rather than a compact card.

- The full-width dossiers alternate paper, oat and botanical-tinted buyer briefs so their visual sequence feels like a curated sourcing journal instead of a repeated administrative template.

- Home category discovery combines full-bleed ingredient imagery with the product names, while the contact page has a subtle crop-to-destination route cue to ensure every key journey feels traceable and provenance-led.

- Dedicated category pages are structured as **quote-led sourcing briefs**: each combines a tactile product image, the buyer use case, clear “available on request” commercial fields, and a direct route back to an enquiry action without inventing product-specific claims.

- Detail pages use a four-step **Meridian handover sequence**—buyer brief, commercial brief, category range and export handover—so traceability and buyer movement structure the full page rather than acting as occasional decoration.

- The leaf-and-S monogram is the primary route seal beside the supplied wordmark, and homepage stages are visibly marked from origin through handover to make the sourcing journey explicit.

- The Gallery is a **visual sourcing journal**, not a generic image grid: uneven editorial apertures, product-route captions and a final supply handover keep every image connected to an origin and buyer conversation.

- Enquiry moments are treated as **Karossy export briefs**: crop, quantity, destination and timing form the visible handover language, while the botanical route seal appears wherever a buyer begins their sourcing conversation.

- The production-template layer uses the leaf-and-S monogram as a restrained Canopy Green and saffron route seal beside the supplied wordmark. Copy prioritises a concrete buyer action, product, season, or destination over generic category claims.

- Every major route-led section now communicates a named sourcing stage through an explicit side spine, waypoint treatment, and stage microcopy rather than using the meridian line as decoration alone.

- Homepage category discovery uses warm, high-saturation crop imagery, varied vertical offsets, and market-selection micro-labels to create a sourcing-journal rhythm instead of an equal-card corporate grid.

- About-page values form an asymmetric **buyer promise sequence**: authenticity, quality, integrity, reliability, customer focus, and growth read as connected handover stages, with the botanical mark language retained at key decision points.
